Nib Rider, round 0

Character sheet and an animated sting prototype. Ink on paper, single-stroke line art with round caps, the same hand as the cursive mark. 2026-10-13. Not committed.

Character sheet

A. The Nib Rider: a musketeer riding the pen like a lance, nib forward, plume streaming. Eyes on you.
B. The nib wears the hat. Simpler, reads at 24 px, but it is the pictogram we are retiring.
C. Bust only. The corner stamp for the app: the face that talks to you.

A is the one for the short. C is what A becomes when it shrinks into the corner of the app, so the character in the video and the assistant on screen are the same person. B is here to show why the pictogram alone is not enough: no eyes, no one to talk to.

Sting prototype

7 seconds. Beats below. Loop with Play.

Beats

1. Forest
0.0 to 0.8 s
Paper ground, two layers of ink tree silhouettes drifting at different speeds. Quiet.
2. The ride
0.8 to 4.0 s
The Rider gallops in from the left on the pen, nib forward, plume and cape in the wind, bobbing at a trot. The nib leaves a looping ink line behind it like handwriting.
3. The word
4.0 to 5.2 s
The Rider exits right. The line settles. Nibwrite rises in Newsreader above a hand-drawn rule.
4. Hello
5.2 to 7.0 s
The bust pops up in the corner, tips the hat, blinks. Hold. This is the same face that lives in the app's corner.

Render path for an MP4: Chrome headless screenshots at 30 fps driven by a small script that steps the CSS animations, then ffmpeg. Sound is one pen scratch and one hat tip; nothing else.
