Questions

Before you ask.

  • Can I write a whole book for free?Yes. Every module, every look, snapshots and compare. Paid plans add sync, hosted Gall, or editorial tools.
  • What happens if I stop paying?Your files stay on your disk exactly as they are. Sync stops; nothing is deleted.
  • Do I need the Gall plan to use Gall?No. His lookups are free and offline, and you can bring your own model key or run a local model.
  • Who is Editorial for?Writers working with an editor, a co-author, or beta readers: shared projects, reviews back from Word, reader rounds.
